Kochi, Kerala

Kochi Intelligence Map

Thirteen neighbourhoods across the city and its islands — Vypin to Thrippunithura, Fort Kochi to Kakkanad. Click a point to open its conditions: weather, seismic activity, air quality, and volcanic risk.

Map is a schematic layout, not a surveyed chart — positions are relative, not to scale.

Weather and air-quality links open a live search card for that area. Seismic activity opens USGS's real-time map for the wider South India region — Kerala sees very few recorded earthquakes. Volcanic activity is included for completeness: there are no active volcanoes anywhere near Kochi.
